Option 6’s spine, option 4’s triage-first grouping, option 6’s tinted-chip pills — plus two semantic corrections the mockups got wrong. Rendered from live state, so every step and pill below is computed, not drawn. Switch states to see the strip react the way the real page does.

State Density

    What changed from the shipped strip

    • Filled done-states. A completed step is a solid green disc with a white check — option 6’s confidence. The shipped version tints the fill, which reads tentative at 18px.
    • Triage-first grouping. Nine flat pills become three questions: what needs me, is quality green, what’s the context. Scan cost drops without losing a single value.
    • “Needs attention”, not “Blockers”. Option 4’s red BLOCKERS header is factually wrong for Pulse: a P1 security finding and a Medium risk score are advisory pressure. Merge is gated by the attested verdict, approvals and the SonarQube gate — nothing else.
    • A “blocks merge” tag that means it. The tag appears only on a pill that genuinely gates — watch the quality-gate pill in the failed state. Findings never get it. This makes the product rule visible instead of implied.
    • Dynamic membership. A pill moves into Needs attention when its own tone turns bad or warn, and moves home when it clears. Try “All settled” — the group disappears entirely.
    • Coverage as an arrow. 86.0% → 98.7% — all six mockups converged on this independently, and it beats the shipped 86.0 B ↗98.7. The grade band moves into the tooltip and the aria-label.
    • Governance stays honest. “3 rules · Enforce”, not option 4’s “3 rules enforced” — Enforce is the repository’s mode, not a property of the rules.
    • No spinner. Option 5 animates the current step, implying something is running when the PR is actually waiting on humans. States here change on events, so a pending step is amber and still.
