Retention & provenance

Applies to change history and decision provenance. Attestation receipts are kept for the life of the workspace regardless of this window.
Record actor and reason on every change A save without a reason is rejected. The reason is stored beside the previous value and the rollback path.
Attach decision provenance to every verdict Selection inputs, replay proof and the signature travel with the verdict — run 06cb9564 carries ed25519:7fd2…9c4e.
Sign policy changes with pulse-auditor Settings changes are counter-signed, so a change history entry can be proven unaltered.
