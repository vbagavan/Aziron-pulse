How replay proof works

On every run, Pulse re-executes a sample of tests the selection model chose to skip — the shadow replay. Recall is the share of would-have-caught failures that selection also caught.

Acceptance criteria: rolling 30-day recall of at least 99.0% per repository. A repository below the bar automatically falls back to broader selection until it recovers — no configuration needed.

Drift telemetry for the underlying model lives in Risk model. Pulse proves — recall is measured by execution, not estimated.
